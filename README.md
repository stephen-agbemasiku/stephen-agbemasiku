## 📌 About Me

I am a professional teacher under the Ghana Education Service and a final-year Master of Philosophy in Economics candidate at the University of Cape Coast. As an African Economic Research Consortium (AERC) Collaborative Masters Programme (CMAP) scholar, my academic training bridges advanced quantitative methods with grounded educational leadership and public policy analysis.

My MPhil thesis research employs empirical econometric modeling to examine the relationship between green finance and sustainable industrial transformation across Sub-Saharan African economies. Alongside my quantitative research, I actively publish policy commentary addressing higher education, curriculum reforms, teacher professional upgrading, and national educational development in Ghana
