## 📌 About Me

I am a professional teacher under the Ghana Education Service and an MPhil Economics candidate at the University of Cape Coast. As an African Economic Research Consortium (AERC) Collaborative Masters Programme (CMAP) scholar, my academic background connects empirical econometrics with public policy and educational governance.

My MPhil thesis research utilizes empirical econometric modeling to examine the dynamics between green finance and sustainable industrial transformation across Sub-Saharan African economies. Alongside my studies, I actively publish commentary on Ghanaian curriculum reform, teacher upgrading policies, and national education development.
